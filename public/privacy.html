Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Big OG Wayne respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you visit BigOGWayne.com, place an order, check an order status, or contact us.

Information We Collect

When you use this site, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address.
  • Order information, such as the items you purchase, product variants, order total, shipping cost, order status, and customer-facing order number.
  • Payment-related information processed through our payment provider. We do not store your full credit card number on BigOGWayne.com.
  • Messages you send through our contact form, including your name, email address, order number if provided, reason for contacting us, and your message.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser/device information or logs needed to keep the site working and secure.

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Process orders and payments.
  • Calculate shipping and submit orders for fulfillment.
  • Provide order confirmation and order-status lookup.
  • Respond to contact-form messages and customer-support requests.
  • Troubleshoot site, checkout, payment, or fulfillment issues.
  • Protect the site from fraud, abuse, spam, or technical problems.

Service Providers

We use trusted service providers to operate the store, including payment processing, order fulfillment, email delivery, hosting, and database services. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their services.

Payment information is processed through Square.

Order production and fulfillment are handled through our fulfillment provider.

Contact-form messages are sent through our email service provider.

Site hosting and database storage are provided through our hosting/database providers.

How We Protect Information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect customer information. Payment details are processed by our payment provider, and we do not store full card numbers on BigOGWayne.com.

No website or online service can guarantee complete security, but we work to keep customer information limited, protected, and used only for legitimate store operations.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep order information as needed for order fulfillment, customer support, accounting, fraud prevention, and business records.

Unpaid or abandoned checkout records may be reviewed, expired, deleted, or anonymized as part of regular site maintenance.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about your order, request help with an issue, or ask questions about your information.

Contact

For privacy questions or customer-support requests, contact us through the Contact page on BigOGWayne.com.
